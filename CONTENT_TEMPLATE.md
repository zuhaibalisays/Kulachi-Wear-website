## Homepage copy guidance

Keep copy:
- short
- confident
- elegant
- product-focused

Avoid unsupported claims.

Good direction:
"New silhouettes, designed to be remembered."

Avoid:
"We are the number one women's clothing brand in Pakistan."

Only use factual business claims supplied by the owner.
